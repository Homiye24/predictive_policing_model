Aim and Objectives of the Research
The aim of this research is to develop an Artificial Intelligence-Based Predictive Policing Model.
The specific objectives of this research are to:
(i)	collect data from Zone 17 Headquarters, Akure, comprising the Ondo and Ekiti State Commands;
(ii)	develop a crime-data enrichment process that extracts relevant information from the Incident Summary field and combines it with structured crime attributes for predictive modelling;
(iii)	develop a hybrid spatial-temporal crime prediction model that integrates a Graph Attention Network (GAT), a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) and Transformer-based attention to predict crime patterns across locations and time periods, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) to identify and interpret the factors that contribute to its predictions; and
(iv)	evaluate the proposed model with appropriate performance metrics and comparative experiments against selected baseline models.
